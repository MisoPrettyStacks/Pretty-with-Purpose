
# Pretty with Purpose 🌸

> *"Beauty in action, purpose in practice."*

**Pretty with Purpose** is a free resource library curated by [MisoPretty (@igotglitteronme)](https://x.com/igotglitteronme) — a growing collection of elegantly designed directories built to help nonprofits, career centers, community organizations, and social service programs access the tools and funding they need to thrive.

Every guide is free. Every resource is verified. No paywalls, no sign-ups, no strings.

🌐 **Live site:** [misoprettystacks.github.io/Pretty-with-Purpose](https://misoprettystacks.github.io/Pretty-with-Purpose/)

### 🎓 Free Certifications & Training Directory — Edition I
**440+ resources · 15 career sectors**

Curated for men and women returning to work after employment gaps — including justice-involved individuals, survivors of homelessness, and persons in recovery. Contains only free, accessible certifications and training pathways across 15 professional fields.

## 💡 Who This Is For

Pretty with Purpose directories are designed for:

- **Nonprofit program directors** sourcing in-kind donations and partnerships
- **Case managers and social workers** connecting clients to free training and certifications
- **Career centers and workforce development programs** serving reentry and recovery populations
- **Community organizations** building resource stacks on zero budget
- **Grant writers and development staff** identifying corporate giving pipelines

## 🤍 Philosophy

This project exists because the organizations doing the most important work — the ones serving people at their most vulnerable — are usually the ones with the least time to find resources and the least budget to pay for them.

Pretty with Purpose is a small attempt to close that gap. Curated with care, formatted for usability, and offered completely free.
